Delete your Msebenzi account

Option 1 - in the app (instant)

  1. Open Msebenzi and log in.
  2. Tap Settings in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2 - by email

If you can no longer open the app, email REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL from the address you registered with and ask for your account to be deleted. We will do so within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your name, email address, bio, skills, profile photo, job photos, device token and alerts are permanently removed and your open jobs are cancelled. Completed jobs and ratings shared with another person are kept without your personal details (shown as "Deleted user").
