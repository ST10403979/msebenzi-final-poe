Msebenzi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Msebenzi ("the app") connects households with local workers. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Msebenzi is a student project built for The Independent Institute of Education (IIE) coursework.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and chosen role (worker or household). If you use Google sign-in we receive your name and verified email address from Google; we never see your Google password.
  • Password: stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We cannot read it.
  • Profile information you add: bio, skills, preferred language and an optional profile photo.
  • Jobs and reviews: job titles, descriptions, locations (text you type), budgets, optional job photos, ratings and review comments.
  • Device token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send you job alerts.

The app does not use GPS, your contacts, your microphone or advertising identifiers, and it contains no ads or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and show your profile to people you do business with.
  • To show jobs, match new jobs to workers' skills and send alerts about them.
  • To show ratings so people can trust each other.

Who can see your information

Other signed-in users can see your name, role, skills, bio, ratings and profile photo, and the jobs you post or accept. Photos are only served to signed-in users. We do not sell your information. Data is stored on our hosting provider (Render) and processed by Google services only for sign-in and push notifications.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. On your phone, your login token is kept in Android's encrypted storage.

Your choices - deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your name, email, bio, skills, photos, device token and alerts, and cancels your open jobs. Jobs you completed with another person are kept in anonymous form ("Deleted user") so their history stays intact. See also how to request deletion.

Children

Msebenzi is not intended for children under 18.
