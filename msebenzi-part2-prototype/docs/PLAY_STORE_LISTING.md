**App name (30 max):** Msebenzi
**Short description (80 max):** Find trusted local work, or trusted local workers, close to home.

**Full description (4000 max):**

Msebenzi ("work" in isiXhosa) connects households with local, reliable workers - fairly and transparently.

🏠 FOR HOUSEHOLDS
• Post a job in under a minute: pick a service, add a photo, set your budget.
• See ratings, reviews and a "Verified" badge before you choose.
• Get an instant alert the moment a worker accepts.

🛠️ FOR WORKERS
• Browse every open job with the price, place and photo visible up-front - no surprises.
• Choose the services you offer and get alerts only for jobs that match your skills.
• Build your reputation with ratings and reviews.

SERVICES WE OFFER
Cleaning • Gardening • Handyman • Painting • Plumbing • Electrical • Moving • Laundry

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE IN SOUTH AFRICA
• Works offline: accept, complete and even post jobs with no signal - Msebenzi syncs automatically when you reconnect.
• Available in English, Afrikaans and isiXhosa.
• Sign in with Google, or with email and a securely encrypted password.
• Delete your account and data at any time from Settings.
